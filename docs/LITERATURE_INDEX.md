# Background literature index

This index adds 82 primary records to the 18 method reviews in the [literature matrix](LITERATURE_MATRIX.md), for 100 distinct works. Each row has one canonical primary link. An arXiv year identifies the reviewed author version. The review level here is primary abstract or paper opening. The method and code audits are recorded in the matrix. The first authors and full linked bylines identify longer author lists. All entries concern action-dependent occlusion, embodied memory, active uncertainty, cooperative perception or the matched motor reference.

## Consequences for the experiments

Tracking and amodal inference motivate measures of visible cue support and association confidence. Contact-valid failures in F2 require the actual outcome to come from observed evidence. F4 scores an identification gap even if the visible locations themselves are clear. A plausible completed image carries its own uncertainty. [Object permanence tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14258) · [Amodal ground truth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.17247) · [Physion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.08261)

Active inspection, localization and successful retrieval have different observations and outcomes. Compare the simulated active branch with stationary RGB at the same clock times. A later motor task can then measure the ability to reach the declared location. [GenMOS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03178) · [Next-best-view classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06766) · [Mechanical Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.01588)

The calibrated policy records belief quality and the coverage of direct decisions. Unresolvable F6 pairs measure whether the system abstains where the same permitted history is compatible with distinct truths. The resolvable control supplies the complementary coverage check. [Proper scoring rules](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf) · [Selective classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08500)

Communication comparisons use one set of admitted cues, charge complete serialized messages, preserve capture and receive timestamps and report delay. Cost-sensitive collaborative perception sets a precedent for an explicit resource comparison. Additional training information needs its own declared scope. [Who2com](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.09575) · [Where2comm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.12836) · [Distilled collaboration graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00643)
