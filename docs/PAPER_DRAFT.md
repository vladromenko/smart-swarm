# No One Saw Everything

*Learning Collective Memory of Hidden Objects*

## Abstract

Robots collaborating on a search can miss different events. In our MuJoCo scenario, SO101 observes a placement attempt, Tank observes the relocation of the containers, and ROSMASTER chooses where to search or requests another observation. We train two causal visual evidence models with the same architecture and compare event supervision with event and terminal-reveal supervision. On 100 independent source worlds spanning six scenario families, both learned selective policies chose the correct first container in 34 of 34 resolvable container cases at each tested team size. They answered all 68 resolving controls and requested inspection on all 32 ambiguous variants per size. Two visual broadcast baselines tied on these endpoints. A matched analysis of the same scenes at eight robots found that selecting witnesses by factor saved 161.6 mean serialized bytes relative to capped compact broadcast while retaining all 34 correct first choices. Changing evaluator truth in 16 ambiguity pairs left both full permitted inputs and predictions unchanged with coupled randomness. The comparison did not establish a benefit from terminal-reveal supervision. In a separate simulated follow-up, two moving-camera images localized the object in seven of eight inspected ambiguity cases versus zero with matched stationary images. Physical validation remains pending. [Independent test results](PRIMARY_RESULTS_INDEPENDENT.md) · [Matched compact replay](PRIMARY_MATCHED_COMPACT_RESULTS.md) · [Moving-camera results](ROSMASTER_ACTIVE_INSPECTION_RESULTS.md)

## Research question and hypotheses

Can a robot correctly choose a search region when placement and relocation are witnessed separately and the intended manipulation can fail?

1. Combining timed private observations of placement and relocation improves the first container choice over receiver-local and single-witness policies under the tested observation and communication protocol.
2. Giving a small evidence model terminal outcome labels during training improves probability calibration and selective decisions.
3. Changing hidden truth while preserving all permitted inputs leaves the coupled pre-inspection prediction invariant. A useful policy also answers matched resolving controls directly.

## Method and dataset

SO101 witnesses a placement attempt with wrist and top RGB. Tank acquires front RGB during changes in the exterior relation. ROSMASTER receives allowed, time-stamped factors and chooses a search region, reports an exposed surface or requests inspection. Each source history has assigned capture and delivery boundaries. The permitted packets, messages, state, caches and timing are fixed for the F6 invariance checks. Evaluator truth and terminal reveals are stored separately and may only enter training and scoring.

In the held-out F1 example `primary-test-f1-00`, the SO101 overhead frame shows the cube in a container at 18.10 s. The Tank's front camera shows the container arrangement at 18.13 s and 23.04 s. ROSMASTER receives the witness messages and its own front image before the query, then chooses the left search region at 23.13 s using the frozen event-only model. Each panel comes from the displayed robot's pre-query replay store. [Three-agent visual histories](../evidence/learning-pilot-v3/primary-example-f1-views.pdf)

The implemented visual backbone is frozen ImageNet ResNet-18 with local `resnet18-f37072fd.pth` weights. The witness heads are two 48-unit GRUs and readout layers with 235,927 trainable parameters in total. The arm head predicts an observed settled placement class and the Tank head predicts the occupied identity permutation. Categorical visual scores feed a log-space update over ten joint states. A validation-selected threshold determines whether the maximum-probability answer is disclosed. The event-only and event-plus-reveal variants share architecture, admitted inputs and splits. Unobserved intermediate labels are masked.

The bounded evidence corpus retained 420 independent source histories and 490 recorded variants. Training and validation use separate source groups. The held-out pilot below is distinct from the independent 100-world primary test.

## Discussion and limitations

The independent primary and frozen one-witness controls support the first hypothesis for the tested cue-marked layout. Both deterministic visual broadcasts matched selective learned decisions on the resolving and ambiguity endpoints. The matched compact controls attribute the measured communication saving to which replies are acquired under the original camera layout. Additional agents reuse the same source scenes and observation opportunity budget. Their inclusion does not establish a general advantage from increasing team size. The viewpoint challenge shows that framing, visual recognition and mount calibration still limit transfer.

Reveal supervision did not establish the predicted benefit. The observed first-choice ceiling, imprecise paired interval and screened F4 errors limit the conclusion. The F6 intervention supports the third hypothesis on the audited pairs. Resolving controls and the centralized RGB counterexample show why invariance is insufficient by itself. The optical follow-up measures location after newly acquired evidence in matched simulation branches and leaves physical motion, physical outcome prediction and retrieval open. ## Reproducibility

The main evaluation packaging records source recipes and assignments, capture and label manifests, checkpoint identities, fixed profiles, sealed per-source predictions and scoring reports. The offline bundle regenerates the principal tables and paired figures, recomputes the viewpoint calibration bounds from saved messages and checks the ACT contact-time comparison against the archived traces. The calibration and primary archives refer to the original Homebrain RGB and encoder by recorded locations. The transfer and moving-camera packages include scoped example exposures. The primary and compact result scripts consume sealed predictions and use the recorded evaluator labels for scoring. [Offline bundle and dependencies](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md#single-package-offline-check) · [Worked three-agent example](../evidence/learning-pilot-v3/primary-example-f1-views.pdf)

Reproduce the reported scores from the local release with the following command. The project-specific commands and paths remain in the protocol. [Per-component commands](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md#per-component-checks)

```bash
MPLBACKEND=Agg PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/archive_paper_evaluation.py \
  evidence/learning-pilot-v3/paper-evaluation-homebrain-v2.tar.gz
```
