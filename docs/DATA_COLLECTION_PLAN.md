## Physical plans awaiting platform integration

- **Pilot stories** target 24 short development worlds, about four per family. Prepare one cube, up to three lightweight open opaque containers, exterior identity cues and a reachable reveal view.
- **Motor preflight** targets 10–20 supervised attempts after verified ownership and STOP. Start with one feasible SO101 placement or retrieval skill. Preserve required camera order, calibration, commanded versus applied targets and the result of the attempt.
- **Study stories** target 160 train and 40 validation histories. Reserve 120 fresh memory-test histories after the protocol is frozen. A separate 24-pair physical comparison awaits feasible execution and matched reset checks.

Confirm camera sequence and timing, common clock uncertainty, robot roles, coverage, action source and reveal target interval before accepting a recording. Quarantine technical interruptions and missing streams. Retain naturally failed physical actions as completed story outcomes when the recording itself is valid.
