# No One Saw Everything

Can an agent locate an occluded cube by combining separate, incomplete visual histories when grasping can fail and a container moves? The B1 rehearsal is an engineering check for this question. The independent analysis unit is the world story and its counterfactual variants share a scene group.

## B1 task freeze

One 32 mm target cube, two opaque open bins, one work surface, three simulators in one shared world and one query. SO101 has wrist and local overhead RGB. Tank has a low camera on the north side and witnesses container exteriors after the manipulation window. ROSMASTER has a low camera on the south side at the query. A narrow color stripe identifies each container. ROSMASTER's view does not show the stripes or cube in these four records. Role and time grants are enforced at capture and again in scoped replay.

- **F1** records a contact grasp, stable carry, release, containment, external slide and query.
- **F2** repeats the intended placement with the cube just outside the closing gripper. The studio camera sees the cube on the surface.
- **F6** withholds SO101's cameras and keeps Tank's exterior view. Two equal-geometry cubes follow the same physical histories. Reset-time appearance assigns the orange target to either the carried cube or the cube on the occluded surface. Both worlds require inspection under the recorded evidence.
- **F3–F5** remain planned. These cover removal or repacking, uncertain associations when exterior cues are ambiguous, and acyclic nesting.

The paired B1 scenes vary cube outcome while preserving the general query layout. A larger control must counterbalance which bin moves and where either identity finishes. The four saved runs form one development scene group and provide no sample of test accuracy. ROSMASTER reports a selection or inspection request. ## Bounded joint state

The location variable has five values: cyan identity, violet identity, work surface, held by the manipulator, and outside the modeled region. The assignment variable has two permutations relating identities to the left and right query slots. All ten combinations are enumerated. Unknown is an inspection decision. The prior has masses 0.42, 0.42, 0.08, 0.04 and 0.04, with equally likely assignments.

Each non-overlapping source clip contributes a conditional relative observation factor. The station cue contributes to location, and Tank's pair of marker observations contributes to assignment. Each factor places weight 0.98 on its selected hypothesis and 0.012 on alternatives. These values are fixed uncalibrated engineering choices. The state posterior is normalized in log space from the prior times the product of admitted factors, then marginalized to query slots. An inspection is requested below 0.75. Deliveries are admitted only after the evidence capture and receive times and by the decision deadline. Retransmitted or overlapping frame origins are deduplicated.

The later learned version should compare a calibrated likelihood-ratio correction using training priors against an explicit observation model. Reveal supervision scores the pre-reveal marginal over the set of compatible states. It must leave the unresolved intermediate paths latent.
